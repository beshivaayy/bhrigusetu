## Evidence policy
- Preserve exact quotations only where legally appropriate; otherwise paraphrase and cite.
- Record title, author, publication date, URL, page/section or timestamp, access date and relevant excerpt/notes.
- Distinguish direct source statements, interpretation, implementation assumptions and empirical observations.
- Do not infer that a general Parashari rule is a BSP rule.
- Source priority does not override contradictory evidence; conflicts are logged in CONFLICTS.md.
