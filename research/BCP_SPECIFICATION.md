Every production mechanism must record: exact rule, author, source, URL, page/section or lecture timestamp, computational condition, timing convention, interpretation, exceptions, confidence and validation case.

The following must be verified from primary/high-priority sources rather than assumed: 12-year cycle mechanics, running-year convention, house/year mapping, cycle ruler terminology, planetary/house interpretation, monthly/sub-period mechanics and exceptions.
