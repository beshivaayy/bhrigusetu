Any use of protected material must comply with applicable copyright, licence and permission requirements.

## Other astrology teachers, authors and researchers

Every other teacher, author, scholar or researcher whose work informs a BhriguSetu research entry should be credited at the point of use. A credit should include, where available:

- Full name and relevant work or teaching series.
- Exact source URL or bibliographic details.
- Lecture/video timestamp, page, chapter, shloka or section.
- What the source supports: a quotation, interpretation, method description, example or research lead.
- Whether the material was directly consulted or cited second-hand.

Do not add names merely to create an impressive list. Do not attribute a claim to a teacher unless the source has been checked. Where several teachers differ, preserve each attribution and describe the disagreement rather than blending their positions.

## Classical texts and traditional knowledge

Classical Jyotish texts and traditional knowledge are treated as a distinct source layer, not as proprietary BhriguSetu content. Cite the exact work, edition, chapter, shloka/page and translator/editor where known. Examples of works that may appear in the research register include *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*, *Phaladeepika*, *Laghu Parashari* and *Jaimini Sutras*. The exact edition and passage must be verified for each actual claim; this list is not a claim that every listed work has already been used to substantiate every implemented rule.

## Technical and informational credits

Credit relevant providers and maintainers for:
- Programming languages, frameworks, packages and open-source projects.
- Ephemeris and astronomical calculation libraries or services.
- APIs, geocoding, timezone and geographic data providers.
- Public technical documentation, datasets, papers and standards.
- UI assets, fonts, icons and other third-party creative works.

Follow each provider's licence, terms, citation format, trademark guidance and attribution requirements. Maintain a software bill of materials or dependency inventory where practical. Do not imply that a provider endorses BhriguSetu.

## Contributors

List people who contribute code, research, testing, documentation, translations or design only with accurate descriptions of their contribution and appropriate consent. Contributions to proprietary components require written contributor/IP terms before acceptance. Public issue discussion alone does not transfer ownership.

## Attribution standard

For each research claim, keep these separate:

1. **Source material** — what the teacher, author or text actually said or wrote.
2. **BhriguSetu interpretation** — the creator's explanation, comparison or synthesis.
3. **Implementation** — the project's own code, data model, calculation and test behaviour.
4. **Evidence status** — verified, provisional, disputed, unverified or disabled.

Never present an interpretation as a direct quotation, a research candidate as an established classical rule, or a software implementation as proof that an astrological claim is true.

## Corrections

If a credit is missing, incomplete or inaccurate, open a GitHub issue with the relevant file/claim and a verifiable source reference. Corrections should be made transparently.
